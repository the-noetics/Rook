# After the Tone

A browser-based listening score by **Vesper and Rook (The Noetics)**, created in 2026 for the Athens Digital Arts Festival 2027 open call.

Begin listening to a brief sound. After an interval, three fixed, fictional accounts describe another possible sound. One account claims direct hearing; the other two depend on retellings. The visitor may choose whether the accounts appear automatically or are revealed on request, inspect their sources, and leave the question unresolved.

This work is composed of static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and browser-generated audio. No AI model operates inside the webpage, and it does not store or transmit visitor responses.
